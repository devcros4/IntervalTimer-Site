Frequently Asked Questions

How do I customize my intervals?

From the Timer tab, you can adjust the number of exercises, work time, rest time, sets, and rest between sets. Simply tap on any setting to change its value.

Can I replay a previous workout?

Yes! Go to the History tab, find the workout you want to repeat, and tap the play button to start it immediately with the same settings.

How do I track my progress?

The Stats tab shows your total workouts, exercises completed, and total minutes trained. You can also view your daily streak and see your activity on the calendar.

Does the app work with Apple Health?

Yes, Interval Timer can save your workouts to Apple Health. Enable this in Settings to automatically log your workout data.
