Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Transparent and simple.

Last updated: January 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Interval Timer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. Interval Timer is designed to function primarily on your device with minimal data collection.

2.1 Personal Data

We do not collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it to us via support channels.

2.2 Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage data to help us improve the app's performance and stability. This includes crash reports and aggregated usage statistics that cannot be traced back to individual users.

2.3 Workout Data

Your workout configurations, history, and statistics are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data unless you explicitly choose to share it with us for support purposes.

3. HealthKit Integration

If you choose to integrate Interval Timer with Apple HealthKit, the app will request permission to save workout data (such as calories burned and duration) to your Health app. This data is handled entirely on your device and is governed by Apple's HealthKit privacy policies. We do not store this health data on our servers.

4. Data Storage

Your workout history, preferences, and statistics are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, this data may be lost unless you have backed up your device.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics or payment processing (Apple App Store). These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Apple App Store for purchases and subscriptions
  • Anonymous crash reporting for app stability

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics collection

7. Children's Privacy

Interval Timer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@intervaltimer.app.
